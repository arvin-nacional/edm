Executive Roundtable: Navigating IT Modernization with OpenShift

Enabling High‑Performance, Future‑Ready IT Platforms

As digital initiatives accelerate, organizations are under increasing pressure to modernize IT environments while ensuring reliability, security, and performance — all without disrupting critical operations.

We are pleased to invite you to a private, in‑person roundtable designed for senior technology leaders who are shaping modernization strategies and platform decisions within their organizations. This session will provide a focused forum to discuss practical approaches to mid‑market IT modernization, grounded in real‑world experience rather than theory.

Through an interactive peer discussion and expert perspectives, the roundtable will explore how Red Hat OpenShift serves as a consistent and scalable foundation for modern application environments, and how Lenovo’s enterprise‑grade infrastructure, tightly integrated with Red Hat software, supports high‑performance, resilient IT platforms.

The session will focus on how organizations are simplifying complexity, modernizing application platforms, and preparing their infrastructure for future demands.

This session will highlight how Red Hat OpenShift provides a proven foundation for modern application environments, and how Lenovo’s enterprise‑grade infrastructure, tightly integrated with Red Hat software, delivers a high‑performance and scalable platform for transformation initiatives.
