Executive Roundtable: Navigating IT Modernization with OpenShift

Enabling High‑Performance, Future‑Ready IT Platforms

As digital initiatives accelerate, organizations are under increasing pressure to modernize IT environments while ensuring reliability, security, and performance — all without disrupting critical operations.

This peer‑focused session will explore practical, real‑world approaches to application and infrastructure modernization using Red Hat OpenShift, supported by Lenovo’s enterprise‑grade infrastructure. Through interactive discussion and expert insights, participants will gain perspectives on how organizations are:

  • Simplifying IT and application platform complexity
  • Building secure, resilient, and scalable environments
  • Preparing infrastructure to support future business demands

This invitation‑only session is designed to foster meaningful dialogue among peers.
